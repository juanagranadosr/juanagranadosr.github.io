<p>This is a small side project I build together with <a href="https://github.com/PaoloGari96" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Paolo Garimberti</a>, we make these to work through ideas we're curious about, one small program at a time. Source for all of them lives in the <a href="https://github.com/juanagranadosr/intuition-lab" target="_blank" rel="noopener">intuition-lab repository</a>.</p>

<div class="card">
  <a class="card-link" href="{{ '/intuition-lab/quantum-gates/bloch-sphere.html' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Open Quantum Gates on the Bloch Sphere"></a>
  <h3>Quantum Gates on the Bloch Sphere</h3>
  <p>An interactive Bloch sphere animating how single-qubit gates (X, Y, Z, H, S, T, and rotation gates) act on a qubit state. Built during the 3rd Workshop of Initiation on Quantum Computing (Universitat de Barcelona).</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <a class="card-link" href="{{ '/intuition-lab/phase-space/phase_space_volume.html' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Open Phase-Space Volume, Liouville, and Attractors"></a>
  <h3>Phase-Space Volume, Liouville, and Attractors</h3>
  <p>Drop a blob of initial conditions into a phase portrait and watch Liouville's theorem, attractors, and phase-space contraction play out. Built as intuition for Fruchart &amp; Vitelli, "Nonreciprocal many-body physics."</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <a class="card-link" href="{{ '/intuition-lab/one-dim-crystal/one-dimensional-crystal.html' | relative_url }}" aria-label="Open The One-Dimensional Crystal"></a>
  <h3>The One-Dimensional Crystal</h3>
  <p>A chain of atoms and springs between fixed walls: pluck, drag, or excite a normal mode directly and watch the spectrum fill up without ever crossing the dispersion ceiling at 2ω.</p>
</div>
