What the model says

Write ξi for how far atom i has moved from its resting site. The spring on its right is stretched by ξi+1 − ξi, the one on its left by ξi − ξi−1. Newton's law is the difference of the two:

m ξ̈i = k(ξi+1 − 2ξi + ξi−1)

The walls enter as ξ0 = ξN+1 = 0. That −2 on the diagonal and the 1s off it are exactly the matrix V = 2IN − B from the problem.

Why modes exist

Nothing in the equation moves one atom alone, yet there are special starting shapes that keep their shape forever and simply breathe in and out. They are the eigenvectors of V:

ξi ∝ sin(nπi / (N+1)),  n = 1 … N

Try mode 1 and then mode 5. Every atom shares one frequency; only the shape changes.

The ceiling at 2ω

Each mode's frequency comes out as

ωn = 2ω sin(nπ / 2(N+1)),  ω = √(k/m)

The sine cannot exceed 1, so no mode of any chain, however long, oscillates faster than 2ω. Raise N to 40 and watch the bars crowd up against the line without touching it. A real crystal with N ≈ 108 fills that band almost continuously.
